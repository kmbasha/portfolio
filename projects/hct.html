Delivered a multi-campus AV/ELV program for Higher Colleges of Technology covering smart classrooms, auditoriums, multipurpose rooms, distance learning environments and campus-wide digital signage. The work was delivered in annual phases while campuses remained operational.

Delivery Environment

The program operated across active education campuses with academic timetables, examination periods, graduation events and campus access windows influencing the delivery sequence. Work had to be coordinated with HCT central administration, campus IT and facilities teams, AV vendors, cabling teams and specialist subcontractors.

Delivery was aligned with UAE Ministry of Education technology standards and the objective of creating a consistent learning and presentation environment across the HCT estate.

Systems Delivered

  • Smart classroom AV systems across 200+ learning spaces
  • Auditorium and multipurpose room presentation systems
  • Crestron and Extron control systems
  • Interactive displays, wireless presentation and audio systems
  • Cisco and Polycom video conferencing environments
  • Distance learning and lecture capture infrastructure
  • Campus-wide background music and public audio zones
  • Centralised digital signage across all 18 campuses

My Role

I managed the end-to-end delivery coordination across all 18 campuses, including rollout planning, phase sequencing, stakeholder engagement, vendor coordination, procurement tracking, schedule control, commissioning oversight and phased handover to campus IT and facilities teams.

My PM Responsibilities

  • Planned phased campus rollout across four annual delivery cycles.
  • Coordinated AV vendors, structured cabling teams, electrical trades and specialist subcontractors.
  • Managed room readiness, installation sequencing and access windows with campus teams.
  • Tracked procurement, long-lead AV equipment, commissioning progress and punch-list closure.

  • Oversaw testing, user orientation, documentation and formal handover for each campus phase.

Delivery Challenge

The main challenge was delivering a consistent AV and distance learning platform across 18 active campuses without disrupting academic operations. Auditoriums, multipurpose rooms and classrooms had different access constraints, technical requirements and usage patterns.

Several systems also needed to work together as one operational environment: AV control, interactive displays, video conferencing, audio, digital signage, network readiness and end-user support.

My PM Approach

  • Used a phased rollout model, with early campuses helping validate installation methods, room standards and commissioning checklists before wider deployment.
  • Separated delivery planning by room type so classrooms, auditoriums, multipurpose rooms and distance learning suites each had clear scope and acceptance criteria.
  • Worked with campus teams to schedule high-impact works around semester breaks, examination periods and major campus events.
  • Maintained consistent standards for AV control, presentation workflow, video conferencing and documentation across all campuses.
  • Managed commissioning and user orientation before handover so each campus could operate the systems with reduced dependency on external support.

Outcome

  • 18 campuses completed through a controlled four-year delivery cycle.
  • 200+ learning and presentation spaces standardised across classrooms, auditoriums, multipurpose rooms and distance learning suites.
  • CPI 1.06 achieved through standardisation and coordinated procurement.
  • 40% reduction in AV support complexity through unified platform standards.
  • 90% reduction in classroom IT support calls within six months of program completion.
  • 500+ inter-campus video conferencing sessions annually enabled across the HCT estate.
  • Full UAE Ministry of Education compliance maintained across all phases.
