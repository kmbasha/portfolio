IKEA Yas Island – Customer Service Centre Technology Upgrade

End-to-end technology upgrade within a live flagship retail environment involving CCTV, control room systems, video wall, digital signage, data centre infrastructure, compliance coordination and operational handover.

IndustryRetail / Commercial
EnvironmentLive Flagship Store
ScaleUSD 1–5M
Duration6 Months
SiteIKEA Yas Island
RoleEnd-to-End Project Manager

Project Overview

This project involved upgrading integrated technology systems for the IKEA Yas Island Customer Service Centre inside a live retail environment. The site was a flagship IKEA store, requiring careful coordination around customer-facing operations, restricted delivery windows, compliance requirements and multiple technical workstreams.

Live retail upgrade Customer Service Centre Control room Video wall Digital signage CCTV Data centre / EMS Night & weekend works

Delivery Environment

  • Technology upgrade inside an operating flagship retail store.
  • Customer-facing business continuity had to be protected during delivery.
  • Work required night and weekend execution windows to reduce operational disruption.
  • Coordination involved MEP contractor interfaces, subcontractors and internal technical resources.
  • Compliance requirements included IKEA standards and local authority expectations.

Systems Delivered

  • CCTV and electronic security infrastructure.
  • ELV room and control room technology coordination.
  • 2 × 4 matrix video wall environment.
  • Bi-lingual digital signage and Customer Experience Management integration.
  • Tier 2 data centre infrastructure and EMS coordination.
  • Structured cabling and network readiness for integrated systems.
  • API integration with CEM platform.

My Project Delivery Role

My role covered end-to-end project management from planning and procurement coordination through installation, subcontractor management, commissioning and handover. The focus was to deliver the upgrade without disrupting live retail operations.

Planning & Site Control

  • Project planning and delivery sequencing
  • Site coordination with retail operations
  • Night and weekend work planning

Commercial & Vendor Control

  • Subcontractor scope coordination
  • Procurement impact tracking
  • Delivery cost control support

Commissioning & Handover

  • System testing coordination
  • Defect resolution follow-up
  • Operational handover readiness

Delivery Challenge

  • Technology upgrade had to be delivered inside a live customer-facing retail environment.
  • Work windows were restricted to nights and weekends to minimise operational disruption.
  • Multiple systems had to be coordinated across CCTV, control room, signage, data centre and CEM integration.
  • MEP contractor interfaces and subcontractor sequencing required close daily coordination.
  • Compliance expectations had to be maintained while keeping the rollout on schedule.

My PM Approach

  • Planned delivery around operational constraints and retail trading requirements.
  • Coordinated subcontractor activities to reduce disruption to customer-facing areas.
  • Managed procurement coordination and material delivery impacts against the project schedule.
  • Maintained regular communication with stakeholders, contractors and technical teams.
  • Controlled delivery risks around access, safety, quality checks and commissioning readiness.
  • Supported final testing, defect closeout and operational handover.

Outcome

The technology upgrade was delivered successfully within the planned rollout period. The project supported improved customer service centre operations, integrated technology readiness and controlled handover within a flagship retail environment, while maintaining business continuity during delivery.

My Project Delivery Takeaway

This project demonstrates end-to-end project management in a live retail environment, including contractor coordination, procurement impact control, weekend/night delivery planning, multi-system integration, commissioning and handover without disrupting customer-facing operations.
