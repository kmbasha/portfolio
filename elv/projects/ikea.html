IKEA Retail Operations & Security Systems Program

Phased retail technology delivery across customer systems, CCTV monitoring, vehicle access control, logistics operations and integrated ICT infrastructure.

Industry
Retail Operations
Scale
200+ CCTV Cameras
Value
Approx. AUD 750K
Delivery Type
Phased Program (~1 Year)

Project Overview

This was a single-site phased retail technology program delivered in a live IKEA environment. The work covered customer service systems, CCTV and VMS upgrade, POS-linked video recording, control room monitoring, vehicle access control, loading bay communication and the ICT infrastructure needed to support those systems.

Live Retail Site Axxon VMS POS Video Overlay Qmatic QMS ANPR & Barriers 3 x 6 Video Wall Structured Cabling

Delivery Environment

  • Single IKEA site with live retail operations continuing during delivery.
  • Customer-facing service areas, delivery pickup, returns, kitchen display and kids section environments.
  • Parking, loading bay and back-of-house logistics operations.
  • Control room monitoring environment with operator-based verification and access release.
  • Night work windows requiring close vendor, safety and security coordination.
  • Regulated security scope requiring authority drawing approval and compliance closeout.

Systems Delivered

  • Qmatic bilingual queue management with token displays and customer flow logic.
  • Digital signage and centrally managed customer display content.
  • 200+ CCTV camera redesign and upgrade with Axxon VMS migration.
  • Redundant VMS servers, storage upgrade and ELV room UPS upgrade.
  • Transaction-linked CCTV video overlay integrated with POS systems.
  • Staff and customer parking barriers, ANPR and access control integration.
  • Loading bay truck barrier, driver intercom and remote video verification.
  • 3 x 6 control room video wall and integrated monitoring environment.
  • Structured cabling and fibre backbone between MDF and IDFs.

My Project Delivery Role

My role covered site delivery leadership, subcontractor coordination, installation sequencing, vendor coordination, WHS-controlled night works, third-party testing and commissioning coordination, defect management and staged handover. Budgeting and procurement were handled outside my direct responsibility.

Site & Vendor Control

  • Night work coordination
  • Subcontractor scheduling
  • Vendor issue resolution

Safety & Access Control

  • WHS induction coordination
  • Work-at-height licence checks
  • Scissor lift operator compliance

Readiness & Handover

  • Testing coordination
  • Commissioning management
  • Defect closeout and handover

Delivery Challenge

  • Most works had to be completed at night to reduce disruption to retail operations.
  • Multiple vendors and specialist contractors needed coordination across CCTV, VMS, QMS, signage, access control and cabling workstreams.
  • Vendor no-shows and reduced site resources affected planned night work windows.
  • Security supervision was required during works in sensitive operational areas.
  • Work-at-height and scissor lift activities required licence and safety control before work could proceed.
  • Security authority approvals were essential because the CCTV system supported IKEA trade licence renewal requirements.

My PM Approach

  • Managed the work as a phased program with separate customer systems, security, access control and infrastructure workstreams.
  • Re-sequenced night work activities when vendors did not provide planned resources.
  • Added recovery days and coordinated additional resources to protect the delivery path.
  • Maintained coordination between store operations, security teams, contractors and third-party specialists.
  • Tracked regulatory drawing approvals, installation readiness, testing, commissioning and handover dependencies.
  • Coordinated specialist testing and commissioning resources rather than performing technical commissioning directly.

Outcome

The phased program was completed with the main systems operational, including CCTV and Axxon VMS, POS-linked video overlay, Qmatic queue management, digital signage, parking and loading bay access systems, control room monitoring and supporting ICT infrastructure. Security regulatory approval was achieved, supporting IKEA’s operational compliance and trade licence renewal requirements.

My Project Delivery Takeaway

This project involved practical delivery leadership across a live retail environment where customer systems, security monitoring, vehicle access, logistics operations and ICT infrastructure had to work together. It is a strong example of integrated technology delivery requiring night work coordination, vendor recovery, safety control, commissioning management and operational handover.
