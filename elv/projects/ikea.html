Project Overview

Led $3.8M integrated technology program across 3 IKEA locations (Yas Island Abu Dhabi, Dubai Festival City, Al Ain) managing 6 concurrent sub-projects: CCTV 500+ cameras, control room, VMS, POS integration, Qmatic CEM, digital signage. Coordinated 30+ team members and 8 vendor partners across 18-month delivery cycle.

My Role

Senior Project Manager responsible for end-to-end delivery coordination across three IKEA locations, including stakeholder management, subcontractor coordination, procurement oversight, vendor integration, schedule governance, compliance management, commissioning, and final client handover.

Key Achievements

  • Delivered turnkey solution on-time and under budget (EVM: CPI 1.04, SPI 1.01)
  • Zero critical defects meeting IKEA global corporate standards
  • Reduced shrinkage by 35% (exceeded 30% target)
  • Full SIRA compliance (UAE Security Industry Regulatory Agency)
  • Successfully supported IKEA internal security audit

Program Management Approach

Hybrid Waterfall-Agile Methodology

  • Infrastructure delivery (CCTV, cabling, control room): Waterfall methodology due to physical construction dependencies
  • POS-CCTV integration software: Agile 2-week sprints for iterative development and testing
  • PMBOK-aligned with emphasis on integration management coordinating multiple concurrent sub-systems

Procurement & Contract Management

  • Developed RFP for security systems incorporating IKEA global security standards and UAE SIRA compliance
  • Evaluated 6 bids using scoring matrix: IKEA-approved vendor status (40%), technical capability (30%), commercial proposal (20%), local support (10%)
  • Negotiated turnkey fixed-price contract ($3.8M) with 12 milestone payments, 10% retention
  • Verified contractor compliance: SIRA installation license, IKEA corporate vendor approval, insurance, safety certifications

Earned Value Management

  • Tracked EVM for $3.8M program: CPI 1.04 (under budget), SPI 1.01 (on schedule)
  • Monthly variance analysis identified CCTV installation 5% ahead of schedule enabling early POS integration testing

Risk Management

Multi-Site Coordination Risk

  • Managed concurrent delivery across 3 geographically separated locations with independent site supervisors and daily cross-site progress reporting
  • Maintained unified risk register across all 3 locations with weekly review cadence escalating site-specific risks to program level
  • Phased delivery sequencing prioritised Yas Island as lead site to validate installation methodology before replication at Dubai Festival City and Al Ain

Live Retail Operational Constraints

  • All installation works planned around IKEA operational hours to avoid disruption to live retail trading
  • Night-shift and weekend installation windows negotiated with IKEA store management for high-impact cabling and control room fit-out works
  • Temporary CCTV coverage maintained during permanent system cutover to ensure zero surveillance gaps

Vendor Delivery Dependencies

  • Long-lead procurement for Matrox video wall controllers and Axis camera stock managed through early purchase orders with buffer stock held on-site
  • Secondary vendor approval maintained for Bosch cameras as contingency against Axis supply delays
  • Vendor performance tracked against contractual milestones with 10% retention released only on satisfactory FAT and SAT completion

Integration Testing Risk

  • POS-CCTV integration identified as highest technical risk — addressed through dedicated Agile sprint testing environment replicating live transaction scenarios before production deployment
  • API failure scenarios documented with fallback procedures agreed with IKEA IT team prior to go-live

Schedule Overlap Mitigation

  • Critical path analysis identified CCTV cable infrastructure as longest-lead activity — mobilised cabling crew 3 weeks ahead of equipment delivery to maintain float
  • Parallel workstream scheduling across control room build, VMS configuration, and POS integration prevented sequential dependency delays

Commissioning & Handover

Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT)

  • Coordinated FAT for VMS servers, Matrox video wall controllers, and Axxon integration platform prior to site delivery
  • FAT sign-off required before equipment release to site — preventing defective hardware reaching live installation phase

Site Acceptance Testing (SAT)

  • Structured SAT programme executed per location covering camera field of view verification, recording quality, motion detection zones, VMS playback, and POS transaction linkage
  • IKEA regional security director and store IT manager present for SAT sign-off at each location

Subsystem Integration Testing

  • End-to-end integration testing across CCTV, VMS, POS, Qmatic CEM, and digital signage confirming all subsystems communicating correctly
  • Simulated transaction scenarios tested across all POS terminals with verified video association before go-live

Operator Training

  • Delivered structured operator training programme for 8 control room operators covering VMS navigation, camera management, incident recording, POS alert response, and Qmatic reporting
  • Training documentation and quick reference guides provided in English and Arabic

Defect Rectification & Punch List

  • Punch list compiled at each location following SAT — tracked to closure before handover certificate issued
  • All Category A defects (operational impact) resolved prior to handover; Category B defects scheduled within 30-day defects liability period

Documentation & Handover Package

  • As-built drawings submitted for all 3 locations including camera layout, cable routing, rack elevations, and network diagrams
  • O&M manuals, warranty certificates, SIRA compliance documentation, and system configuration records compiled and handed over to IKEA facilities team
  • Handover meeting conducted with IKEA regional security director confirming acceptance of all 3 locations
