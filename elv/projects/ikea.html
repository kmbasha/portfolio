Project Overview

This program involved phased delivery of integrated retail technology systems for a live IKEA site. The scope crossed customer-facing systems, electronic security, control room monitoring, vehicle access control, loading bay operations and ICT infrastructure supporting CCTV, intercom and queue management environments.

Live Retail Site CCTV / Axxon VMS Qmatic QMS POS Video Overlay ANPR / Barriers Structured Cabling

Delivery Environment

  • Single IKEA retail site with live customer operations.
  • Customer service, delivery pickup, returns and internal service areas.
  • Staff parking, customer parking and loading bay vehicle access points.
  • Control room monitoring environment with operator-based verification.
  • Night work delivery windows to reduce impact on trading operations.
  • Work performed under security supervision with WHS induction and licensing controls.

Systems Delivered

  • Qmatic bilingual queue management and customer flow systems.
  • Digital signage, customer display screens and centrally managed CMS content.
  • 200+ CCTV camera redesign and upgrade with Axxon VMS migration.
  • Redundant VMS servers, storage upgrade and UPS upgrade in the ELV room.
  • Transaction-linked CCTV video overlay integrated with POS systems.
  • 3 x 6 control room video wall for monitoring and operator verification.
  • Parking and loading bay barriers, ANPR and remote intercom operation.
  • Structured cabling and fibre connectivity between MDF and IDFs.

My Project Delivery Role

My role covered site delivery coordination, subcontractor management, installation sequencing, vendor coordination, live-site planning, defect management and staged handover. Specialist testing and commissioning were completed by third-party experts, with my role focused on coordinating readiness, access, sequencing, issue resolution and closeout.

Site & Vendor Coordination

Safety & Access Control

  • Testing readiness coordination

Customer & Retail Operations Systems

  • Bilingual Qmatic queue management supporting language, service and payment journey selection.
  • Token systems and display screens for customer service, delivery pickup and returns areas.
  • Queue and signage displays across customer-facing areas including kitchen and kids sections.
  • Digital signage displaying IKEA promotional content together with queue information where required.
  • Centrally managed CMS setup for Qmatic and customer display content.

Security, Monitoring & Vehicle Access

  • Full CCTV design upgrade with regulatory drawing approval and security compliance support.
  • Axxon VMS migration with redundant servers, storage upgrade and UPS support.
  • POS transaction data overlay linked to CCTV recording for cash register video review.
  • Staff parking ANPR integrated with access control for automatic barrier operation.
  • Customer parking barriers and separate loading bay truck barrier.
  • Loading bay driver intercom with control room video verification and remote barrier release.

Delivery Challenge

  • Most work had to be completed at night to reduce disruption to live retail operations.
  • Vendor attendance and resource shortfalls created delivery pressure during planned work windows.
  • Security-supervised working conditions required careful access and work sequencing.
  • WHS induction, work-at-height requirements and scissor lift operator licensing had to be controlled before site work.
  • Multiple systems had to be delivered in parallel across customer areas, parking, loading bay and ELV infrastructure.

My PM Approach

  • Coordinated phased workstreams around night access windows and live operational constraints.
  • Managed vendor coordination, attendance issues and resource shortfalls by adjusting work plans and adding recovery days where required.
  • Sequenced cabling, ELV room readiness, CCTV installation, queue management displays, barriers and intercom works.
  • Coordinated third-party testing and commissioning specialists across VMS, CCTV, Qmatic, barriers and intercom systems.
  • Supported closeout of defects, regulatory readiness items and operational handover requirements.

Outcome

  • Security regulatory approval was achieved, supporting the site’s operational licence renewal requirements.
  • CCTV, VMS, POS overlay and monitoring systems were commissioned and handed over.
  • Qmatic queue management and customer display systems were deployed for customer service and related service areas.
  • Parking, ANPR, loading bay barrier and remote intercom systems became operational.
  • Delivery disruptions caused by vendor resource shortfalls were managed through revised sequencing and additional planned work windows.

My Project Delivery Takeaway

This program demonstrates integrated retail technology delivery across AV, electronic security, customer operations, vehicle access and ICT infrastructure. It required live-site coordination, night work planning, vendor control, WHS compliance, third-party commissioning coordination and practical handover management in a customer-facing retail environment.
