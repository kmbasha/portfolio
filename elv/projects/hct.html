Project Overview

The project involved delivery execution for a large-scale government education AV rollout managed from Abu Dhabi HQ, covering campuses across the UAE. The scope included AV automation and classroom technology across more than 200 learning spaces, including classrooms, auditoriums, amphitheatres and multi-purpose rooms.

Multi-Campus Rollout AV Automation Crestron Control Classrooms & Auditoriums Commissioning & Handover

Delivery Environment

  • Government education environment with multiple campus stakeholders.
  • Central coordination from Abu Dhabi HQ with delivery across UAE regions.
  • Parallel site delivery streams across classrooms, auditoriums and specialist teaching spaces.
  • Coordination with subcontractors, technical teams, consultants and client representatives.
  • Remote coordination with Crestron programmers and DSP configuration resources.

Systems Delivered

  • AV wiring and classroom technology infrastructure.
  • Crestron control systems for classroom and room automation.
  • ClearOne DSP, Yamaha amplification and Extron switching.
  • Remote Crestron programming and DSP configuration coordination.
  • NEC projectors and Panaboards across learning environments.
  • Commissioning, testing and staged operational handover.
