UiPath RPA Financial Services APRA Compliant Audit Support 6 Months 5 BOT Types Agile Delivery

Project Overview

Managed the final implementation and handover of a UiPath RPA program for Virgin Money Australia — a regulated Australian financial services organisation. The engagement covered a 6-month delivery phase implementing 15+ automation bots across 5 process categories, automating 5,000+ transactions per month. Delivery was conducted within a heavily regulated environment requiring APRA prudential standards compliance, comprehensive audit trail management, and formal change control throughout.

My Role

Project Manager responsible for end-to-end delivery coordination of the RPA implementation and handover program. Responsibilities included stakeholder management with Virgin Money business and IT leadership, coordination of the UiPath vendor team and internal business process owners, Agile sprint governance, change control management, APRA compliance oversight, audit documentation, UAT coordination across business units, and formal handover to Virgin Money operations and support teams.

Key Achievements

  • 15+ RPA bots delivered across 5 process categories within 6-month program timeline
  • 85% reduction in manual processing time across document processing workflows — exceeded 80% target
  • Zero processing errors post-deployment across all automated processes
  • ROI achieved within 8 months — significantly ahead of the 12-month business case projection
  • Successfully supported Virgin Money internal audit with zero findings
  • Staff redeployed from manual processing to customer-facing roles — improving customer satisfaction by 12%
  • Full APRA prudential standards compliance maintained throughout delivery and post-handover

Delivery Approach

Agile Sprint Governance

  • Program structured into 2-week Agile sprints — each sprint delivering a defined set of bot configurations, integration completions, and UAT sign-offs
  • Sprint planning conducted with Virgin Money business process owners and UiPath technical team to prioritise automation candidates by business value and implementation complexity
  • Sprint review sessions held with Virgin Money IT and business stakeholders at end of each sprint — confirming acceptance of completed bots before proceeding to next sprint scope
  • Backlog maintained and updated throughout program — scope changes managed through formal change request process to protect delivery timeline and budget

Process Sequencing and Dependency Management

  • Five process categories sequenced in delivery order based on business priority, integration complexity, and downstream dependencies — Document Processing and Account Reconciliation prioritised as foundational automations enabling subsequent bot categories
  • Integration dependencies with Virgin Money core banking systems mapped upfront — IT environment readiness confirmed before each bot category entered development and testing
  • Parallel workstreams managed for bot development, UAT environment setup, and APRA documentation to prevent sequential delays

Change Control and Scope Management

  • Formal change control process applied throughout — all scope additions, process definition changes, and integration requirement updates assessed for schedule and budget impact before approval
  • Virgin Money IT change management procedures followed for all system integration touchpoints — change requests raised, approved, and scheduled through the client's change advisory board (CAB) process
  • Scope baseline maintained against original business case — variance reporting provided to Virgin Money project sponsor at fortnightly steering meetings

Stakeholder Coordination

Virgin Money Business Leadership

  • Fortnightly project steering meetings with Virgin Money project sponsor and senior business stakeholders — covering delivery progress, sprint outcomes, risk status, compliance posture, and budget tracking
  • Executive reporting maintained in plain business language — focused on outcomes delivered, business value realised, and decisions required rather than technical implementation detail

Business Process Owners

  • Individual business process owners engaged per bot category — responsible for process definition documentation, UAT participation, exception scenario validation, and business sign-off before production deployment
  • Process definition workshops facilitated at the outset of each bot category to confirm current-state process maps, exception handling requirements, and success criteria before development commenced

Virgin Money IT Team

  • IT infrastructure and security teams engaged for environment provisioning, system access approval, network configuration, and security assessment of bot credentials and system touchpoints
  • Integration requirements coordinated with core banking system owners — API access, data exchange formats, and system availability windows agreed before each integration development sprint

UiPath Vendor Team

  • UiPath technical resources coordinated through weekly delivery meetings — tracking development progress, blocking issues, integration test outcomes, and environment dependencies
  • Vendor deliverable quality reviewed against agreed acceptance criteria before UAT handover — rework managed through vendor without impact to client-facing milestones where possible

Risk Management

Regulatory and Compliance Risk

  • APRA prudential standards applicable to automated financial processing identified as primary program risk — mitigated through early engagement with Virgin Money compliance team to define audit trail requirements, data handling controls, and change management obligations before development commenced
  • AML/CTF obligations reviewed for all bots processing customer account and transaction data — compliance controls embedded in bot design rather than applied retrospectively

Core Banking System Integration Risk

  • Integration with live banking systems identified as highest technical delivery risk — mitigated through dedicated non-production test environments replicating live system behaviour before any production deployment
  • Rollback procedures documented for each bot prior to production deployment — enabling rapid reversal to manual processing if post-deployment issues arose

Business Process Change Risk

  • Risk of in-flight process definition changes invalidating completed bot development — managed through process freeze agreements with business process owners once development commenced, with any changes subject to formal change control and sprint re-scoping

Staff Adoption and Change Impact

  • Staff impacted by automation redeployment identified early — change impact assessment conducted with HR and business managers to plan communication, retraining, and role transition ahead of each bot go-live
  • Proactive communication to affected teams emphasising redeployment to higher-value customer service roles rather than workforce reduction — supporting adoption and reducing resistance

Schedule Risk

  • 6-month delivery window identified as tight for 15+ bot implementations — mitigated through parallel sprint workstreams, early resolution of environment and access dependencies, and weekly risk review to surface blocking issues before they impacted the critical path

Compliance & Audit Management

APRA Prudential Standards

  • All automated processes designed and documented to meet APRA prudential standards applicable to financial services technology — covering operational risk controls, system resilience, data integrity, and audit logging
  • Bot credential management, access controls, and exception handling procedures documented to APRA audit standard throughout delivery

Audit Trail and Documentation

  • Comprehensive audit trail maintained for all bot executions — transaction logs, exception records, and processing confirmations retained in line with Virgin Money's record-keeping obligations
  • Project documentation maintained throughout delivery including requirements traceability, test evidence, change records, and sign-off artefacts — packaged for handover to Virgin Money compliance and IT teams

Internal Audit Support

  • Virgin Money internal audit conducted during program delivery — provided full project documentation, compliance evidence, bot configuration records, and access logs within agreed timeframe
  • Zero audit findings — all controls confirmed operating as designed

UAT Coordination & Go-Live Management

  • UAT plans developed per bot category with Virgin Money business process owners — defining test scenarios, acceptance criteria, exception cases, and sign-off authority
  • UAT environments provisioned and managed separately from production — all bot configurations validated in test before any production deployment request raised
  • Go-live approvals obtained through Virgin Money's change advisory board process — deployment windows scheduled for low-risk periods avoiding month-end and regulatory reporting cycles
  • Hypercare period of 2 weeks maintained post each bot go-live — UiPath vendor and PM available for rapid response to production issues before transitioning to standard support

Handover & Support Transition
