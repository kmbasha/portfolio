Project Overview

This work covered new Tier 2 and Tier 3 data centre builds for banking environments, together with more than 10 critical server room and data centre relocations across large enterprise, manufacturing and healthcare clients.

The delivery scope combined power, cooling, ICT infrastructure, structured cabling, security, environmental monitoring, connectivity services, approved drawings and operational cutover coordination.

Tier 2 / Tier 3 Banking Data Centres Server Room Relocations Power & Cooling Fibre & Structured Cabling SD-WAN / Carrier Services Commissioning & Handover

Delivery Environment

  • New data centre builds for banking clients, including two Tier 2 facilities and one Tier 3 facility.
  • Server room and data centre relocations for manufacturing, healthcare and large enterprise environments.
  • Delivery under large main contractors and civil contractors, with authority approval cycles coordinated through contractor channels.
  • Weekend cutovers and short stabilisation windows where critical infrastructure had to be moved, reconnected, validated and handed back to operations.
  • Banking environments requiring Central Bank, fire and safety approval pathways before operational acceptance.

Systems Delivered

  • Electrical infrastructure, UPS systems, smart PDUs, switchgear, power distribution and generator coordination.
  • Precision cooling / CRAC environments, DX air-conditioning, raised floors, hot/cold aisle containment and environmental monitoring.
  • Rittal racks and cabinets, Panduit and 3M structured cabling, LMZ cabling inside data centre environments and fibre backbone between MDF and IDF rooms.
  • Cisco switching and routing, Brocade storage switching, Dell / HP server environments and EMC storage infrastructure.
  • APC and Newave UPS platforms, APC air-conditioning environments and related power / cooling support systems.
  • Telephony and voice services, dedicated ISP services and SD-WAN supporting secure inter-bank connectivity and Central Bank communication requirements.
  • Access control, CCTV, FM200 fire suppression, fireproof painting and physical security integration.

New Data Centre Builds

The new-build work focused on banking data centre environments where Tier 2 and Tier 3 infrastructure had to be delivered with controlled approval cycles, coordinated contractors and staged commissioning.

  • Worked with main contractors, MEP teams, civil contractors and specialist subcontractors.
  • Coordinated delivery across power, cooling, cabling, racks, security, fire systems and ICT infrastructure readiness.
  • Followed approved drawings and regularly updated drawing revisions through the approval cycle.
  • Coordinated contractor execution against approved drawings, site conditions and client operational requirements.

Critical Server Room Relocations

Relocation projects involved controlled dismantling, labelling, transport, reinstallation, rewiring and validation of live enterprise IT infrastructure.

  • Relocated racks, servers, switches, routers, storage, UPS systems and DX air-conditioning support equipment.
  • Labelled cables and equipment before dismantling to support accurate rebuild at the new location.
  • Backtracked unidentified cabling and services with stakeholders where existing labels were incomplete or missing.
  • Completed physical relocation during weekend windows, with stabilisation and stakeholder validation over the following days.

Delivery Challenge

  • Maintaining uptime and reducing downtime risk during relocation and cutover activity.
  • Working within weekend execution windows where infrastructure had to be moved, reconnected and stabilised quickly.
  • Managing equipment and cable identification where approximately 20% of existing cabling was not properly labelled.
  • Coordinating with business stakeholders and technical owners to backtrack services before dismantling equipment.
  • Managing contractor dependencies across electrical, cooling, cabling, security, fire systems and ICT infrastructure.

My PM Approach

  • Prepared relocation sequencing around business downtime windows and stakeholder availability.
  • Enforced labelling, documentation and cable identification before dismantling racks and equipment.
  • Coordinated with stakeholders and third parties for validation of services after reinstallation.
  • Tracked drawing revisions, approved issue drawings and approval cycles during data centre builds.
  • Coordinated specialist teams for testing, commissioning, defect closure and final handover.
  • Managed authority approval dependencies through contractors, including Central Bank, fire and safety approval pathways.

Outcome

The data centre builds and relocation works were completed with operational handover achieved across banking and enterprise environments. Critical cutovers were coordinated through planned execution windows, stakeholder validation and post-move stabilisation. Required approvals, testing coordination, commissioning support and handover activities were completed through the contractor and client delivery process.
