Project Overview

Managed the enterprise-scale rollout of a GPS-enabled mobile attendance platform across a distributed workforce of 15,000+ workers spanning construction sites, 250+ retail locations (Jashanmal Group, Burberry), and healthcare facilities across the UAE. The program involved mobile device deployment across dispersed field and operational environments, enterprise ERP and payroll system integration, and operational transition from manual attendance processes to a fully automated, GPS-verified workforce management platform.

The rollout was delivered across multiple client environments simultaneously — each with distinct operational workflows, HR systems, and workforce characteristics — requiring concurrent project coordination, stakeholder management, and integration sequencing across construction, retail, and healthcare sectors. The platform replaced manual attendance reconciliation processes that had generated significant payroll inaccuracies and compliance exposure across all client organisations.

My Role

Project Manager responsible for end-to-end coordination of the mobile attendance platform rollout across all client sites and workforce segments. Responsibilities included stakeholder engagement with operations, HR, payroll, and IT leadership teams across multiple client organisations; rollout planning across geographically distributed construction sites, retail locations, and healthcare facilities; vendor and subcontractor coordination; mobile device provisioning and MDM deployment coordination; UAT coordination with business process owners; operational transition planning; workforce orientation coordination; and handover and ongoing support transition management.

Key Achievements

  • 15,000+ workers enrolled onto the mobile attendance platform across construction, retail, and healthcare environments
  • Eliminated buddy punching — saving $400K+ annually across client organisations through GPS-verified attendance records
  • 70% reduction in attendance processing time across all client payroll teams
  • 95%+ workforce adoption within 2 weeks of go-live at each deployment site
  • ROI achieved within 9 months across the program portfolio
  • Successful ERP integration across SAP, Oracle, PeopleSoft, Dynamics, Zoho, and Odoo platforms without payroll processing disruption
  • 29+ Android devices deployed and managed under Microsoft Intune MDM across field and operational environments

Delivery Approach

Phased Workforce Rollout Strategy

  • Program structured by client sector and workforce segment — construction sites mobilised first as highest-complexity environments, followed by retail locations, then healthcare facilities
  • Within each sector, a phased deployment approach used: pilot site → controlled expansion → full rollout — enabling operational issues to be identified and resolved before scaling to remaining locations
  • Each client organisation treated as a distinct delivery workstream with its own rollout schedule, stakeholder engagement plan, integration timeline, and go-live milestone

Pilot Deployment Strategy

  • Pilot deployments conducted at a representative sample of each client's workforce locations — covering at least one construction site, one retail cluster, and one healthcare facility before full-scale rollout commenced
  • Pilot phase used to validate device configuration, GPS accuracy, ERP integration data flows, exception handling, and workforce adoption behaviour under real operational conditions
  • Pilot findings formally reviewed and incorporated into full rollout configuration before wider deployment — reducing post-go-live defects and adoption friction across remaining sites

Mobile Device Provisioning Approach

  • 29+ Android devices (Samsung Galaxy XCover ruggedised series, Huawei rugged devices) provisioned centrally before field deployment — enrolled into Microsoft Intune MDM, application installed, GPS and network policies configured, and device testing completed before site handover
  • Employee buses from labour camps equipped with tablets for construction workforce — device placement, mounting, and connectivity confirmed per bus route before operational go-live
  • Device readiness checklist completed per deployment batch — confirming MDM enrolment, application version, GPS function, and network connectivity before devices despatched to field locations

Integration Sequencing

  • ERP and payroll system integrations sequenced per client — integration development, testing, and UAT completed before any client workforce was migrated from manual to automated attendance processing
  • Parallel operation period maintained for each client during transition — both manual and automated attendance records produced simultaneously for an agreed validation period before manual process was decommissioned
  • Payroll reconciliation review conducted at end of parallel operation period with client HR and payroll teams before final cutover sign-off

Governance and Reporting Cadence

  • Weekly project status meetings per client — covering deployment progress, integration status, device performance, open issues, and upcoming go-live milestones
  • Fortnightly program-level reporting across all client workstreams — tracking consolidated progress, risk status, and delivery performance
  • Agile 2-week sprint cadence applied to integration development and mobile application configuration — sprint reviews conducted with client IT and HR stakeholders to confirm acceptance before production deployment

Stakeholder & Vendor Coordination

Operations & Site Management Teams

  • Site supervisors and operations managers engaged as primary field stakeholders — responsible for workforce mobilisation coordination, device distribution logistics, and on-site adoption support
  • Operations briefings conducted per site before go-live covering device operation, GPS check-in procedures, exception handling, and supervisor reporting access

HR & Payroll Departments

  • HR and payroll team leads engaged as key integration stakeholders — responsible for validating attendance data flows, approving payroll integration outputs, and confirming parallel operation results before manual process decommission
  • Payroll cutover sign-off obtained from HR directors at each client organisation before manual attendance processing was retired

Client IT Teams

  • Client IT teams engaged for ERP integration access, network connectivity provisioning at operational sites, MDM environment coordination, and security assessment of the mobile platform
  • IT infrastructure readiness confirmed per site before device deployment — network coverage, WiFi availability, and cellular connectivity validated for field locations where GPS devices were deployed

Workforce Coordinators & Supervisors

  • Workforce coordinators at construction and retail locations trained as first-line platform support contacts — reducing dependency on central helpdesk for routine operational queries post-go-live
  • Supervisor access to attendance dashboards and exception reports configured and validated before go-live at each location

Device & Platform Vendors

  • Mobile device vendors (Samsung, Huawei) coordinated for ruggedised device procurement, firmware specifications, and warranty support arrangements
  • Platform vendor technical resources coordinated through weekly delivery meetings — tracking integration development progress, testing outcomes, and configuration deliverables
  • Vendor performance tracked against sprint commitments — blocking issues escalated immediately to prevent integration timeline slippage

Risk Management

Workforce Adoption Resistance

  • Adoption resistance from workers accustomed to manual sign-in processes identified as primary operational risk — mitigated through structured workforce briefings in workers' primary languages before go-live, clear communication from site supervisors, and a supervised parallel operation period removing pressure of immediate full adoption
  • Buddy punching elimination framed to supervisors as a compliance and payroll accuracy initiative rather than a surveillance measure — supporting management endorsement and workforce communication

Payroll Integration Dependency Risk

  • Payroll system integration failures identified as highest business-impact risk — mitigated through extended parallel operation periods, payroll reconciliation reviews before cutover, and documented rollback procedures to manual processing for each client
  • Integration testing conducted with production-representative payroll data volumes — edge cases including shift changes, overtime, leave, and public holidays all validated before go-live

Mobile Device Provisioning Delays

  • Ruggedised device procurement lead times (4–6 weeks) managed through early purchase orders — buffer stock maintained to absorb site expansion requests without delaying scheduled go-lives
  • Device failure contingency plan maintained — replacement units pre-enrolled in MDM and available for same-day field swap

Connectivity Limitations at Field Locations

  • Construction sites and remote operational locations surveyed for cellular and WiFi connectivity before device deployment — offline attendance capture mode configured for locations with intermittent connectivity, with automatic sync on reconnection
  • GPS accuracy validated per site — locations with GPS shielding (basement carparks, enclosed facilities) mapped and alternative check-in methods configured where required

Attendance Data Integrity Risk

  • Data integrity between mobile platform, middleware, and ERP systems validated through reconciliation checks at each integration layer during parallel operation — discrepancies investigated and root-caused before cutover approval
  • Audit trail maintained for all attendance records — enabling dispute resolution and payroll query support post-go-live

Multi-Site Rollout Coordination Challenges

  • Concurrent deployments across construction, retail, and healthcare environments with different operational rhythms — managed through site-specific rollout schedules, dedicated site coordinators, and weekly cross-site status consolidation
  • Retail locations (250+ sites) sequenced in geographic clusters to enable efficient device distribution, supervisor training, and on-site support resource deployment

Technical Scope

  • Microsoft Intune MDM: Enterprise device management, application deployment, security policy enforcement, and remote device management

Commissioning & Handover

Mobile Application & Device Validation

  • Each device batch validated before field deployment — confirming MDM enrolment, application version, GPS function, network connectivity, and battery performance under operational conditions
  • GPS accuracy tested per deployment location — check-in coordinates validated against known site boundaries before workforce go-live

ERP Integration Testing

  • End-to-end integration testing conducted per client ERP — attendance records generated on mobile platform traced through middleware to payroll system outputs, confirming data integrity at each integration layer
  • Exception scenarios tested including shift variations, overtime, missed check-ins, and public holiday calculations — confirmed handling correctly before production cutover

Attendance Workflow Validation

  • Complete attendance workflows validated with client HR and payroll teams during UAT — covering standard check-in/out, supervisor overrides, exception handling, leave integration, and reporting outputs
  • UAT sign-off obtained from client HR directors before production go-live at each organisation

User Orientation & Training

  • Workforce orientation sessions conducted per site — covering GPS check-in procedures, device handling, exception reporting, and supervisor contact for issues
  • Supervisor and HR team training sessions conducted covering attendance dashboard, exception queue management, payroll export procedures, and first-line support responsibilities
  • Quick reference guides produced per workforce segment in English and Arabic

Defect Tracking & Closure

  • Post-go-live defect tracking maintained per client — operational issues categorised by severity and tracked to closure within agreed response timeframes
  • Critical defects (payroll impact, GPS failure, MDM connectivity loss) escalated immediately with same-day resolution target

Operational Handover & Support Transition

  • Handover documentation package prepared per client including: platform configuration records, integration specifications, device inventory and MDM enrolment records, user guides, and vendor support contacts
  • Client IT and HR operations teams transitioned to steady-state support — internal support procedures documented and first-line support contacts confirmed before PM engagement concluded
  • Post-implementation review conducted with each client confirming delivery against business case, lessons learned, and identification of further automation or integration opportunities
